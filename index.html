How to play

  • Swap two neighbouring jacks (drag, or tap one then the other) to line up 3 or more of the same color in a row or column.
  • Match 4 for a blast jack that clears its row or column. Match 5 for a rainbow bola that wipes a color.
  • Tap the floating ball (or press Toss! / Space) to throw it up. Until it lands you can swap and match as many times as you can — quick back-to-back matches build a combo bonus. When it lands the turn ends and you tap it again for the next toss.
  • Reach the goal before you run out of tosses. In higher levels only the glowing colors count.
  • Stuck? Tap Hint. Tap ? any time to see this again.
